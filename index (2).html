Final-year project

Simusolve: AI-Driven Fault Detection System

A Digital Twin of industrial equipment for observing parameter changes and detecting faults without affecting the physical system.

  • Processes temperature, vibration, pressure, current, voltage and load data.
  • Random Forest model detects and classifies faults such as sensor drift, abnormal vibration and pressure fluctuations.
  • Health-monitoring dashboard with anomaly alerts and maintenance recommendations to reduce unplanned downtime.

A browser-based simulation for observing brake-pressure behaviour and detecting abnormal conditions.
